FAQ

Common questions

What is an NFC card?

A card with a small chip inside. Tap it on a phone and it opens whatever link you choose, such as your website or reviews page. No app is needed.

Do NFC cards work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, with most modern iPhones and Android phones. A few older phones or thick metal cases may not read them.

What does website maintenance include?

A monthly check of your site, a backup, keeping an eye on your security certificate and domain renewal, and up to 2 small text or photo edits a month. New pages and redesigns are quoted separately. On Silver and Gold there is a 6-month minimum, then you can cancel with one month's notice.

How long does a website take?

Usually 1 to 7 days, depending on how complex the site is. We confirm the timescale in your quote. The clock starts once we have your content (text, logo and photos).

Can I buy a mix of services?

Yes. You can buy any service on its own, or ask us for a quote that combines several.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain and hosting are set up in your name, and we are given access to look after them. Once you have paid in full, the website design and content are yours, so if you stop working with us, the website stays with you.

Is there a contract for Silver and Gold?

Silver and Gold are monthly subscriptions with a 6-month minimum. After that you can cancel any time with one month's notice. Bronze is a one-off payment.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer or card. Websites, videos and designs need a 50% deposit to start, with the rest due before your website goes live, before final video files are sent, or before designs go to print. NFC card orders are paid in full when you order.

Do you print and fit van graphics?

Yes. We design your van and uniform logos ourselves, and arrange the printing (and fitting for vans) for you. You approve a proof before anything is printed.

Where do you work?

Anywhere in the UK. Websites, designs and NFC card set-up are done remotely. Filming can happen wherever you need it. Every filming job is quoted separately, because the price depends on the location and filming time. Travel for filming is charged at 80p per mile, counted there and back, and added to your invoice. This includes the videos in the Gold package.
