What we agree
before starting.

  • We begin with a defined trial and agree checks before using the agent with customers.
  • The agent uses approved information and actions. Your team handles requests outside those limits.
  • Bookings, orders and quotes require separately tested connections and your permission.
  • The proposal covers phone and software charges, information handling and ongoing review.

Your questions, answered.

What makes the agent personalised?

Its role, business information, tone and permitted actions are tailored to your business. We explain the software, access and ownership arrangements in the proposal.

Can it answer our business phone?

That is one possible role. We would first check your phone setup and trial tasks such as answering common questions, taking messages and passing requests to your team.

Does it learn everything automatically?

No. It starts with the information and instructions you approve. We agree how updates and corrections will be reviewed.

How would we know if it is helping?

Before the trial, we choose what to measure: for example, accurate enquiry details or time saved on a repeated task. We review those results and the running costs with you.
