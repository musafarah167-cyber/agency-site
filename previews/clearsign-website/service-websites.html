Example project

A salon or barbershop

A customer is looking for a haircut and wants to check your services before booking.

  1. 1

    They find your services, prices, opening times and location on their phone.

  2. 2

    They follow the booking link or send your team an enquiry.

  3. 3

    Your booking system confirms the appointment, or your team replies to the request.

The aim is to help someone go from finding your business to arranging a visit without hunting for information.

What we agree
before starting.

  • The quote sets out the pages, content, revisions and contact options included.
  • A booking request is not a confirmed appointment. We check how your booking system handles confirmations.
  • Hosting, domain access and maintenance are agreed before launch.
  • Search-engine setup can be included. Rankings and a particular number of enquiries are not guaranteed.

Your questions, answered.

Can you improve our existing website?

Yes. We first check the current website, your access to it and what you want to keep. Then we can recommend changes or discuss a rebuild.

Do we need all the words and photos ready?

No. You will need to confirm your business details and services, but we can work through the content with you. Filming is quoted separately if you need it.

Can the website connect to our booking system?

A link to your existing booking page is often enough. For a closer connection, we check what your booking system supports and test how appointments are confirmed.

What happens after launch?

We agree the handover, access and any ongoing maintenance with you. Updates outside that agreement can be quoted separately.
