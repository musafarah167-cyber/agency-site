## Evidence and limits

The final visitor-flow.json records 150 passing assertions and 80 layouts, no browser script errors, failed assets or external requests; the same report records all dimensions. Both Chrome and WebKit passed the updated enquiry flow. At 390×844, every service enquiry button ends between 444px and 511px, within the first screen. visitor-text-zoom.json records the independent 20-layout clipping check; earlier passing reports belong to earlier revisions.

This is a source and local-browser review, not a real customer study. Physical devices, software keyboards and screen-reader sessions have not been checked. Conversion performance and willingness to buy have not been measured. The contact form deliberately sends and stores nothing. Real business details, tested enquiry delivery, final public scope and production checks remain launch work; no deployment is authorised by this task.
