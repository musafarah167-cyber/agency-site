The ClearSign logo

Primary logoSymbol + wordmark

One recognisable mark.

The C comes from ClearSign. Its amber square makes the symbol recognisable on a small screen or a business card. The name does the explaining; the symbol makes it easier to remember.

Amber leads.
Charcoal supports.

Amber marks the main action, the logo’s separate square and selected highlights. Charcoal carries the words and the body of the mark. Warm white keeps the page open and readable.

Supporting colours: muted text #65655F; separators #D8D6CE. Use amber deliberately for primary actions, highlights and small progress details, with charcoal text on amber surfaces. For small amber text on light backgrounds, use the darker shade #835600. Colour must not be the only way to communicate a status.

Headlines adapt to the available space. Body text uses a line height of 1.5–1.7 and stays within roughly 65 characters per line. Keep labels legible; don’t shrink important information to make it fit.

Sound like people
who know the work.

Use plain British English. Name the service, explain what it does and give a concrete example. Keep promises within what we can deliver.

Be specific

Instead of “Unlock your business potential”.

“A website that shows your services and makes it easy to enquire.”

Explain the technology

Instead of “An intelligent, seamless ecosystem”.

“Keep customer enquiries, notes and follow-ups in one place.”

Don’t invent proof

Instead of promising a percentage increase in sales.

Explain what a proposed solution would do, and label examples as examples.

Give the logo
room to work.

Use the supplied files. Keep the proportions and spacing intact, and choose the version that contrasts with its background.

Clear space

Leave at least the width of the detached square around the visible artwork. Keep text, borders and other marks outside that space.

Small sizes

Start at 24px for the symbol, 150px wide for the primary logo and 220px for the full signature. The 16px sample is a small-size check, not the preferred size.

Keep the shape

Don’t stretch, rotate or rearrange the logo. Keep the separate square in place. Use the full signature only when “Solutions” is easy to read.

Keep the background simple

Use a supplied colour version on a quiet surface. Don’t add outlines, gradients or shadows to the flat logo, or place it across busy imagery.
