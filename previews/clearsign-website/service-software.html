Your questions, answered.

What kinds of software could you build?

We can discuss job trackers, customer portals, reporting tools and connections between systems. Start by telling us what the team needs to do.

Do we actually need custom software?

You may not. We check whether a suitable existing product can be set up for the job before recommending a new build.

Can we start with something small?

Yes. Starting with one process makes it easier to test the idea and decide what would be worth adding later.

Who looks after it once it is built?

Your proposal sets out hosting, access and the support arrangement. We agree maintenance and future changes with you before starting.
