The enquiry is just the beginning.

A website brings someone to your door. Connected systems help your team take the next step — and remember the one after that.

  1. 01 / Capture

    An enquiry arrives.

    A customer asks about hiring a car. Your website collects the vehicle, dates and contact details, so your team has something useful to work with.

    Example enquiryExecutive saloon · Friday–Sunday

    “Is this available, and what would it cost?”

  2. 02 / Organise

    The details stay together.

    The enquiry goes into one customer record. The hire team can see what was requested, who is handling it and what needs checking.

    Example customer recordAssigned to the hire team

    Next step: check availability and prepare a quote.

  3. 03 / Follow up

    Your team knows who needs a reply.

    Once a quote is sent, a reminder keeps the enquiry on the team’s radar. Someone can follow up while the customer is still deciding.

    Example reminderFollow up on the quote

    Check whether the customer needs anything else to decide.
