Help with enquiries while your team is busy.

A personalised AI agent is a digital assistant set up with your business information. It can answer routine questions, take messages or help prepare follow-ups. We start with one task and a supervised trial.

What you get.

Give routine requests a first response and pass useful details to your team. Your staff stay responsible for decisions, exceptions and anything outside the agent’s agreed task.

Discuss this service

A phone assistant

Trial answers to routine questions and collection of names, numbers and messages. We check your phone setup and agree how callers reach a person.

A website enquiry assistant

Help visitors find approved service information and collect the details needed for a reply. We agree which questions it can handle.

A quote-follow-up assistant

Help prepare a follow-up using an existing quote and customer record. Your team approves the wording and any rules for sending; the agent does not invent prices.

Your information and clear limits

Choose one of these jobs to start. You approve the information, tone and allowed actions, including when the agent must take a message or hand over.

A supervised trial and review

Test ordinary questions, difficult cases and failed connections together. Agree the checks, monitoring and support needed before wider customer use.

Example project

A team handling phone enquiries

Your staff are busy when someone calls with a question or asks for a callback.

  1. 1

    The agent answers questions using the business information you have approved.

  2. 2

    It collects the caller’s details and a short description of what they need.

  3. 3

    Your team receives the request and handles anything that needs a person’s decision.

The aim is a useful first response while staff are busy. The trial checks accuracy, message delivery and human handover before deciding whether it is suitable for wider use.

Common questions

What makes the agent personalised?

Its job, answers, tone and allowed actions are set up for your business. Your proposal also explains your access and what you own.

Can it answer our business phone?

We can trial that after checking your phone setup. We start with common questions and messages, and agree how callers reach a person.

Does it learn everything automatically?

No. It starts with the information and instructions you approve. We agree how updates and corrections will be reviewed.

How would we know if it is helping?

We agree what to measure before the trial, such as accurate messages or time saved. Then we review the results and running costs with you.
