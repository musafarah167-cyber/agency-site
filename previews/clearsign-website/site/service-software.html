Common questions

What is a customer portal?

A page where customers sign in to see the updates or documents you choose to share. We agree what they can access and how your team keeps it up to date.

Do we actually need custom software?

You may not. We check whether a suitable existing product can be set up for the job before recommending a new build.

Can it show our business performance?

It can include a reporting view if the relevant data is available. We agree a few useful measures, such as outstanding jobs, turnaround time or job costs, and check that the figures mean what you expect.

Who looks after it once it is built?

We agree that before building. Your proposal explains where the tool runs, who can access it, who provides support and what future changes may cost.
