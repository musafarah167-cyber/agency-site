Example project

A car-hire company

Someone asks about a vehicle for a weekend, and your team needs to check availability.

  1. 1

    The enquiry form collects the vehicle, dates and customer’s contact details.

  2. 2

    The request is saved in your customer records and assigned to a team member.

  3. 3

    A reminder prompts that person to check availability and send a quote.

The team can see who owns the enquiry and when to follow up. Your staff still check availability, set the price and confirm any hire.

Common questions

Will we have to replace our current tools?

Not necessarily. We check them first. Better settings or a connection between your existing tools may do the job. If you need a completely new tool, we can discuss custom software separately.

What does CRM mean?

CRM stands for customer relationship management. In practice, it is a shared place for contacts, enquiries, quotes and next steps, so your team can see who needs a reply.

Can you automate follow-up messages?

We can assess that with you. You approve the messages, recipients and timing, including when sending should stop or a person should take over.

What happens if a connection stops working?

We plan and test for that. Before launch, we agree how your team will be alerted, who will investigate and which actions should wait.
