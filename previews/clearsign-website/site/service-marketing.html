What you get.

Make it easier for people to recognise your business, understand what you do and find a way to contact you. Use consistent details across your online and physical materials.

Discuss this service

Filming and editing

Short videos for your website or social pages, showing your business, services or finished work. We agree what to film and deliver.

A plan for the shoot

We agree the location, people and work to film. Your quote includes the filming time, travel and finished videos.

Tap-to-open business cards (NFC)

Tap the card against a compatible phone to open your website, booking page or other chosen link. Plain and custom designs are available.

Branded clothing

Designs for shirts, polos, caps and other agreed workwear. We arrange printing through suppliers and confirm quantities and materials before ordering.

Van graphics

Vehicle graphics designed for your business, with printing and fitting arranged through a partner.

Example project

A local business showing its work

You want people who see your work or meet your team to remember the business and know how to contact you.

  1. 1

    Plan and film a short video showing your work, people or services.

  2. 2

    Prepare the agreed video formats, along with any cards, workwear or vehicle graphics.

  3. 3

    Use the same business details and website link across the finished materials.

The aim is a more recognisable business with clear routes to its website or contact page. The proposal can cover one item or a coordinated set of materials.

How we check
it helps.

Agree who you want to reach, what you want them to do and where the material will be used. Review the response alongside production costs and the work it brings in.

A consistent business identity

Check that the name, logo, colours and contact details match across the agreed videos, cards, clothing and vehicle graphics.

Useful attention

Review available video views, profile visits or link activity in context. Choose the measures that relate to the audience you want to reach.

Enquiries and booked work

Ask how people found you and review enquiry or booking activity where it can be recorded. Views and card taps alone do not show revenue.

What we agree
before starting.

  • Your quote lists the filming, travel, edits, changes and finished files included.
  • Printing and fitting depend on confirmed materials, quantities and supplier availability.
  • For tap-to-open cards, we agree the link and check it works before preparing your cards.
  • Paid advertising, daily social account management and a full brand-identity package are outside this service.

Common questions

Can we book a video without buying a website?

Yes. You can ask for filming, cards or another service on its own. We can also discuss combining them for a project.

What does an NFC card do?

It opens a link on a compatible phone when tapped. That could be your website, booking page or review page. We agree the destination with you before preparing it.

Do you print and fit van graphics yourselves?

We handle the design and arrange production. A partner handles printing and fitting, with the details confirmed for your vehicle.

Can you film outside Birmingham?

We can discuss filming elsewhere in the UK. Travel, location and production requirements are included in your quote.
