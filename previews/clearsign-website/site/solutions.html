AI agents

The phone rings while your team is busy, or the same questions keep interrupting the working day.

An AI agent is a digital assistant set up with your business information. A phone, website enquiry or quote-follow-up assistant can start with one agreed task and a supervised trial.

Discuss this serviceExplore AI agents

What you get

  • Answers based on business information you approve
  • Enquiry details and callback requests for your team
  • Rules for when the agent must pass a request to a person

What it helps with

Routine enquiries can get a first response while your team focuses on the work that needs a person. We review accuracy, handover, time and running costs during the trial.

Admin & follow-up

Details are spread across emails, messages and spreadsheets. Someone has to remember who needs a reply.

We organise customer details and set up repeated tasks to happen automatically. For example, a form can save an enquiry and create a reminder to reply.

Discuss this serviceSee how we simplify admin

What you get

  • Contacts, enquiries and follow-up tasks in one place
  • Forms that pass details into your customer records
  • Reminders and customer updates with wording and timing you approve

What it helps with

Your team can see who owns each enquiry and which follow-ups are due. Compare admin time and missed steps before and after connecting the tools.
