# Ideas and advice (not decisions)

Suggestions from Claude. Move an item to DECISIONS.md once the owner agrees.

- Push Silver as the main package: monthly maintenance is repeat income.
- Check Bronze covers its cost: 4 plain cards at £30 is £120 before any website work.
- Quote Platinum by message because it includes a van design.
- Keep video in Gold and above (it takes the most time).
- Show the monthly price next to every package that includes maintenance.
- Build two or three clearly labelled demo sites (plumber, roofer) to show prospects.
- Later: one page per service, local SEO pages, Google Business Profile, a simple "how NFC works" video.
- Later: use an email form service so enquiries also arrive by email.
- Match colours to the logo once one exists.
- Outsourcing: show the customer a mock-up before anything is made, and keep the partner's price list private. Test with one small paid job before promising turnaround times on the site.
- As a sole trader you are personally liable for mistakes, so insurance matters more. Ask insurers about cover for work done by partners.
