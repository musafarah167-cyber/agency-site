## Problems, numbered, with recommendations
1. **FAIL, print (design/front end).** The fade-in (`.reveal`, added by script to sections that are below the screen) leaves them at 0% opacity until scrolled into view, and there is no print rule. Printing or "Save as PDF" without scrolling gives blank sections. Fix: add `@media print { .reveal { opacity: 1 !important; transform: none !important; } }`. Evidence: 12 / 11 / 10 sections invisible in print mode.
2. **FAIL, keyboard (front end, `site/script.js` finder `show()`).** After "Start again" the button you pressed disappears and focus drops to the page body, so keyboard and screen reader users lose their place. Fix: when restarting, focus the first option of question 1 (the code skips focusing when `n === 1`). Also recommended: a "Back" button on questions 2 and 3 (today the only way back is to finish and restart).
3. **MEDIUM, design: "try it" phone screen is clipped.** The fake phone's last button ("Leave a review") is cut off at the bottom at 1280, and at 375 "Website" and "Leave a review" are cut and the card's "Tap here" is cramped against the card edge. See `1280-nfc-tools-used.png`, `375-nfc-tools-used.png`. Fix: make the phone screen taller or shrink its content.
4. **LOW, design:** the finder result "(6-month minimum)" breaks at the hyphen on phones ("(6-" then "month minimum)"). Use a non-breaking hyphen or `white-space: nowrap`.
5. **LOW, front end:** finder "Just NFC cards" pre-selects "Custom NFC cards" in the quote form even if the person wants plain. Suggest "Not sure yet" or leave unchanged.
6. **LOW, design:** at 768 the animated phone in the hero briefly rises from behind the slanted bottom edge (part of the animation, not a fault). The van at the far right is also cut by the slant as it leaves. No action needed; noted so it is not a surprise.
7. **LOW, design:** the web mock-up caption sits tight against the phone mock-up; the video reel mock-up overlaps the end of the progress bar. Cosmetic only.
8. **NOTE (front end):** with JavaScript off the quote form, calculator, finder, menu and demo do nothing (phone, WhatsApp and email links still work). The mobile menu button would show but not open on a phone. Rare; mention only if "works without JavaScript" is ever claimed.
9. **Phase 3 still open:** `noindex` on staging, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, canonical, `og:url`, absolute https `og:image`, LocalBusiness data, privacy and terms pages, host serving `404.html`.
