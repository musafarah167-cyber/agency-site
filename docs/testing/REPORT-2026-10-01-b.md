## Findings for the other sessions
1. **FAIL, design (`site/styles.css`): reduce-motion does not fully stop the hero.** With "reduce motion" forced on, 33 animations still run: 30 twinkling windows and 3 road trails. DECISIONS says everything stops. Cause: the reduce-motion rule uses `.cs-win`, `.cs-trail`, which is weaker than the rules that start them (`.cs-windows .cs-win` at line ~289 and `.cs-trail.t1/.t2/.t3` at lines ~294 to 296), so they win. Fix: use the same selectors in the reduce-motion block (or add `!important`). The SVG-script pause itself works (`animationsPaused() = true`).
2. **Low, design: the "Get paid" screen covers part of the caption** "Example custom card design for a made-up business" at desktop width during part of the loop (screenshot `1280-hero.png` shows "...for a mad"). DECISIONS requires that card to be labelled as an example. Also the "New enquiry, Quote request, just now" screen has no "example" label of its own; it could read as a real enquiry. Suggest moving the caption below the animation, or adding "Example" on the screen.
3. **Low to medium, design: lots of animation on phones.** 66 animations run on phones even though the trailer screens are hidden (windows, bokeh lights, signs). Suggest: switch off bokeh and window twinkle below 820px, and pause the hero animation when it is scrolled out of view (IntersectionObserver or `animation-play-state`). Worth testing on a real mid-range Android phone.
4. **Low, design: spacing.** In the Why section at 1280px the heading sits almost touching the first row of cards (about 20 px), tighter than other sections.
5. **Phase 3 still to do (front end):** `noindex` on staging, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, canonical, `og:url`, absolute https `og:image`, LocalBusiness data, privacy and terms pages, host serving `404.html`.
