# Monthly maintenance checklist (static sites, internal)

For customer sites on a maintenance plan. Copy per customer per month and keep the copies outside the repo.
**Included:** up to **2** small edits a month (confirmed 2026-09-30); new pages and redesigns excluded. Only do what the customer's plan includes.

Customer: [NAME]   Site: [URL]   Month: [MONTH YEAR]   Done by: [WHO]

## Every month
- [ ] Site loads on desktop and phone; no browser console errors.
- [ ] Every page and menu link works (no dead links).
- [ ] `tel:` and WhatsApp buttons still go to the right number.
- [ ] Quote/contact form still works: send a test enquiry and confirm it arrives.
- [ ] SSL certificate valid and not close to expiry (write the date).
- [ ] Domain renewal date checked (write the date; remind the customer well before).
- [ ] Hosting account fine (no warnings, no unpaid invoices).
- [ ] Backup: copy of the site files saved to [WHERE].
- [ ] Contact details, opening times, prices and offers still correct: ask the customer if anything changed.
- [ ] Speed check on mobile.
- [ ] Any new third-party parts (form service, embeds) still working and still needed.
- [ ] Update dependencies only if the site uses any (static sites often have none).
