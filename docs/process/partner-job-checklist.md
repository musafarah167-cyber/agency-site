# Partner job checklist (outsourced printing and fitting)

For every uniform or van job. We design in house; the partner prints (uniforms and vans) and fits (vans). The customer deals only with Clear Signs, so if the partner gets it wrong, the customer will still come to us. Each step below protects us. Keep the filled copy OUTSIDE the repo.

## Before the first job (once per partner)
- [ ] Written price list, minimum order, turnaround times. [OWNER: obtain]
- [ ] Sample work seen; small paid sample job done first.
- [ ] Partner's public liability insurance seen (copy or certificate, expiry date [   ]).
- [ ] Written agreement on who pays if the job goes wrong (reprint, damage to a van): [DECISION PENDING]
- [ ] Our own insurer told we use a partner; cover for their work confirmed [   ] (see `docs/business-setup-guide.md`).
- [ ] Partner agrees what customer details they may receive and will not contact our customers directly or keep data longer than needed (see privacy draft).
- [ ] Our margin on their price decided: [DECISION PENDING, TODO section C].

## Per job: before sending anything to the partner
- [ ] Customer's written approval of the final proof saved (date [   ]). **Nothing goes to the partner without it.**
- [ ] Deposit received, covering at least the partner's cost (rule from PLAN.md risk 4).
- [ ] Van jobs: make, model, year, size, panel condition and dated photos recorded (`customer-onboarding-checklist.md`).
- [ ] Partner's written quote for this job matches our quote to the customer (with our margin): £[   ]
- [ ] Print-ready files, colours (reference codes), sizes and quantities sent in one message; partner replies to confirm exactly what they will make: [saved]
- [ ] Delivery or fitting date agreed in writing (estimate only, tell the customer it is an estimate).

## During and after
- [ ] Partner sends a photo or sample of the printed item before delivery or fitting, when possible.
- [ ] Compare against the approved proof (spelling, colours, sizes, quantity).
- [ ] Van fitting: photos of the van before and after, damage noted at handover and signed off by the customer on the day.
- [ ] Any fault: photos, note date, tell the partner in writing the same day, tell the customer what happens next.
- [ ] Customer confirms in writing that the job is complete.
- [ ] Balance received. Partner invoice checked and paid by [date]. Record in the sales/expenses sheet.

## If it goes wrong
Stay calm, keep everything in writing, tell the customer promptly and honestly, and follow the terms (section 13). Do not admit legal liability in messages before you have checked your insurance and agreement with the partner.
