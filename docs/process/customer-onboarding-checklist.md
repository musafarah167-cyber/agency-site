# Customer onboarding checklist

Internal. Fill in a copy per customer and keep it OUTSIDE the repo (it holds personal data). Tick only what the customer has actually confirmed. Nothing is assumed.

## For every customer
- [ ] Name, business name, trading address or contact address for the invoice: [   ]
- [ ] Phone, WhatsApp, email: [   ]
- [ ] Business or individual (consumer)? [   ] (rules differ, see terms)
- [ ] Services wanted and packages: [   ]
- [ ] Quote sent and accepted in writing; deposit received.
- [ ] Terms sent and acknowledged **[once checked by a professional]**.
- [ ] Privacy notice link given **[once published]**.
- [ ] **Portfolio consent (opt-in, in writing):** The customer has said "yes" in writing to us showing this work, and what may be shown (name, logo, screenshots, photos, quote text): [   ]. Default is NO. Date: [   ]. They can withdraw at any time.
- [ ] Any reviews or testimonials supplied are real, with the customer's name and written permission to use them.

## NFC cards (blank or custom)
- [ ] Blank or custom; quantity; delivery address.
- [ ] Custom: artwork/logo files supplied; spelling and colours checked.
- [ ] Link the card opens: [URL/phone/WhatsApp/etc]. Customer controls or owns it? [DECISION PENDING: Q15]

## Website
- [ ] Brief completed (`website-build-process.md`): pages, services, areas, opening hours.
- [ ] Logo, photos and text supplied, and they confirm they own or may use them.
- [ ] Domain and hosting: who owns them **[DECISION PENDING: default customer owns domain/hosting]**; existing accounts or new; access method (never put passwords in the repo).
- [ ] Existing site or email to protect before switching.
- [ ] Maintenance wanted? Which plan **[X]**.
- [ ] Approval of the staging site recorded in writing.
